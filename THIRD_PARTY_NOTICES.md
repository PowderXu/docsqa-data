# Third-party sources

This collection preserves material and provenance from the following projects.
The exact revisions and content roots are recorded in `sources.json` and in the
dataset manifest.

- GitHub Docs: https://github.com/github/docs
- Tailwind CSS documentation: https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss.com
- Prisma documentation: https://github.com/prisma/web
- Supabase documentation: https://github.com/supabase/supabase

Original community questions and accepted answers retain their per-record
source links and are attributed to their original authors through those links.
Model-assisted reference answers and aspect annotations are identified as
derived annotations in the schema and README.

Upstream text and code retain their applicable licenses and notices. This
repository does not apply a blanket license to third-party documentation or
community contributions. Repository access does not itself grant additional
rights to upstream content.
